# poppy
This widget is free for your own websites and blogs. Share a Poppy to support the cause.

This widget will automatically trigger each year between the 21st October to the 12th November, in line with the Poppy Appeal of the Royal British Legion.

# embed
## embed on html websites and blogs
```
<!-- Poppy Widget -->
<!-- Author: Warren Woodhouse (T-Codes) - warrenwoodhouse.blogspot.com/codes -->
<!-- Documentation: https://warrenwoodhouse.github.io/poppy -->
<iframe src="https://warrenwoodhouse.github.io/poppy/button/" allowtransparency="true" frameborder="0" width="550" height="550"></iframe>
<p><small>Widget By: <a href="https://warrenwoodhouse.blogspot.com/codes">T-Codes</a> on <a href="https://warrenwoodhouse.blogspot.com/">Warren Woodhouse</a></small></p>
```

# disclaimer
I would like to thank everyone who has installed or used this widget, you’re helping veterans and their families of those who have served in the armed services here in the United Kingdom and it’s oversea territories.

My dad is a veteran and every single day I look up to him for his smile and honesty.

No part of this widget is intended to make profit nor do I wish to make any, I only want to help those where I can and where I am able to do so. This widget will support those in need and help remember those who have died for peace.

I would like to extend my appreciation, respect and acknowledgment for the Royal British Legion and all forms of armed services here in the United Kingdom and oversea territories for their continued efforts to bring peace upon our nation.
